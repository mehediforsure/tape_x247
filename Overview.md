## Overview

Tape_x247 is an independent platform dedicated to discovering, documenting, preserving, and connecting electronic music and underground cultures across South Asia, including Bangladesh, India, Pakistan, Nepal, Sri Lanka, and the broader diaspora.

Part publication, part living archive, part discovery platform, and part creative laboratory, tape_x247 aims to make overlooked artists and sounds more discoverable while encouraging connections between musicians, listeners, and creative communities.
It is an evolving project, shaped as much by the communities it encounters as by the people building it.

## Core Identity and Philosophy behind the project

- It is a cultural platform combining music discovery, independent journalism, curation, community, and experimentation.

- Focuses on music discovery, cultural context, art & artists appeciation and human curation rather than competing with other streaming or news/media platform.

- Continuous Transmission (x247) - An evolving archive of music, stories, artists, and ideas.
- Community-Driven - A home for emerging and overlooked artists, with opportunities for collaboration and participation.
- And, the most important thing about Tape_x247 is its Open-Ended. Which means the platform should evolve organically without being restricted to a predefined set of features.

## Principles

- Prioritize artistic expression and discovery over popularity or commercial success.
- Preserve underground music histories through accurate documentation.
- Represent South Asia's diverse musical cultures without generalization.
- Encourage meaningful connections across geographical and musical boundaries.
- Favor thoughtful curation over algorithm-driven engagement.
- Experiment with design and technology without compromising usability.
- Keep the platform independent, accessible, and open to future possibilities.


## Core Sections(Current)

- Discover: Curated releases, artist recommendations, genre exploration, and discovery by location9(South Asian Region), mood, or musical connections.
- Editorial: Interviews, reviews, scene reports, essays, photo essays, and independent music journalism.
- Featured: Interconnected profiles of artists, releases, labels, collectives, events, venues, and regional scenes.
- Learn/Experimental Lab/Then Try This: Interactive music experiences, creative coding, digital exhibitions, educational resources, and music production tools.

Upcoming: 
- Community: Artist submissions, guest mixes, event updates, collaborative projects, listening sessions, and community contributions.


## Development Guidelines

- Preserve the existing architecture and design language unless changes are requested.
- Build incrementally; avoid implementing speculative features prematurely.
- Keep artists, releases, labels, events, and editorial content interconnected.
- Prioritize responsive design, accessibility, and performance.
- Never fabricate any information or publish unverified content as fact.
- Keep the code modular, maintainable, and easy to extend.
