# tape_x247: South Asian Electronic Music Archive & Zine

## Overview
tape_x247 is a digital magazine, zine, and living cultural database dedicated to the underground and electronic music landscapes across South Asia (including Bangladesh, India, Pakistan, Nepal, Sri Lanka, and the broader diaspora).

## Core Identity
* **Not a streaming service:** Functions as a hybrid between a cultural database (Discogs, Resident Advisor, Wikipedia) and an underground print/digital zine.
* **The "Tape" Metaphor:** Rooted in physical audio culture, cassette archiving, mixtapes, and underground transmission lines that bypass mainstream gatekeepers.
* **Continuous Flow (_x247):** A 24/7 archive, continuous stream of new releases, live session recordings, and daily dispatches from various metropolitan and regional scenes.

## Core Features & Sections
* **Scene Reports & Dispatches:** Ground-level journalism covering DIY spaces, warehouse parties, underground clubs, and bedroom producer communities from Lahore to Colombo, Kathmandu to Mumbai, and Dhaka.
* **Oral Histories & Archival Features:** Deep dives into forgotten electronic experiments, early synth/experimental music pioneers in South Asia, and diaspora sonic lineages.
* **Artist Spotlights & Mix Series:** Exclusive guest mixes, Bandcamp deep-dives, and producer profiles paired with structured metadata.
* **Artist Profiles & Discographies:** Cataloging regional artists and their releases.
* **Release & Label Archives:** Tracking independent labels operating regionally.
* **Event & Festival Documentation:** Documenting underground parties, festivals, and live showcases.
* **Search & Discovery:** Filtering by genre, city, and mood, mapping connections between artists, labels, and communities.
* **Editorial Content & Oral Histories:** Interviews, photo essays, liner notes, and tour diaries.
