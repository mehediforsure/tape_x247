About the field guide.

This is an independent learning project by Mehedi, inspired by the concepts in the Ableton Live 12 User Manual. The explanations, graphics, and sounds here are original. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ableton.

These are deliberately simplified teaching models. Browser-generated sounds illustrate timing, pitch, envelopes, and routing; they do not reproduce Live’s instruments or proprietary Warp algorithms. The Session lab uses a 4/4 clock with fixed 120 BPM and a shared one-bar loop.
